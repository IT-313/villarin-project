Our Story

The essence Cebu, refined.

Villarin Scent & Co. was born from a singular vision: to capture the soul of the Visayas and elevate the rich aromas of our Bisaya heritage to the pinnacle of modern luxury. We believe that a fragrance is more than a mere accessory, it is an invisible garment, a lingering memory, and a quiet statement of elegance.

Drawing inspiration from the lush landscapes of the South, we meticulously blend indigenous botanicals with world-class ingredients. Proudly rooted in Mandaue City and Cebu, our fragrances honors the unhurried patience of our ancestors. Every bottle we craft is a tribute to the authentic Visayan spirit, designed for the modern individual who commands presence without saying a word.

Our Philosophy

  • ➵
    Authentic Heritage Rooted in Bisaya culture, celebrating the local festiveness, evocative coastlines, and timeless stories of the South.
  • ➵
    Uncompromising Quality Slow-macerated blends using premium, hand-selected native resources and island spices.
  • ➵
    Modern Elegance Tender profiles that stay close to the skin and leave an enigmatic trail.
